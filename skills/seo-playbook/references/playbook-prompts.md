# Playbook prompts and message templates

Copied from The Local SEO Playbook (Stolos Digital). Fill the bracketed parts with known facts; write `[FILL IN]` for anything you don't know. Never invent volumes, prices, reviews or quotes.

## 3.1 Pick the main keyword

```text
I run a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] in [CITY].
My main services are: [LIST].

Suggest the 5 search terms a local customer is most likely to type
into Google when they need this business, in the form
"[category] [city]". Rank them from most to least likely and say in
one line why. Do not invent search volumes.
```

## 3.6 De-duplicate competitors' services

```text
I'm pasting a list of services from competitors.

Your task:
1. Remove all exact duplicates.
2. Remove all near-identical variants of the same term. Keep only ONE
version of each: choose the most common or professional form, but do
NOT edit or reword it beyond selecting one from the list.
3. Do NOT add, remove, combine, rephrase or limit characters in any
other way.
4. Do NOT group, categorise or format. Output only the final clean list,
one service per line, nothing else.

List:
[PASTE YOUR LIST HERE]
```

## 3.6 Sort services under categories

```text
My Google Business Profile has these categories: [CATEGORY 1, CATEGORY 2, ...].

Assign each service below to the single category it fits best.
Do not rename, add or remove services. Output one heading per category
with its services underneath, one per line.

Services:
[PASTE YOUR MASTER LIST]
```

## 3.6 Write the top-20 service descriptions

```text
I need a service description for each item in the top 20 below.
Each service description has to be 300 characters or less.

Business: [TYPE OF BUSINESS] in [CITY].
Top 20 services:
[PASTE YOUR TOP 20]
```

## 3.8 Generate profile FAQs

```text
Write 6 frequently asked questions with short answers (under 300
characters each) for a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] in [CITY].
Cover what local customers really ask before they call: services,
areas served, prices or quotes, timing, booking, parking or access.
Use only these facts about the business: [FACTS].
If a fact is missing, write [FILL IN] instead of guessing.
```

## 4.1 How do I change this on the platform?

```text
The website runs on [PLATFORM, e.g. WordPress with Yoast / Wix / Webflow].
Give me step-by-step instructions to change:
1. the homepage SEO title tag,
2. the H1 and H2 headings on the homepage,
3. the site-wide footer text.
Tell me where each setting is in the current interface and how to
check the change is live.
```

## 6.4 Citation and backlink ideas

```text
Can you suggest some backlinks and citation sources I could get for a
[NICHE] in [LOCATION]? Include local directories, industry
directories, associations and local news or community sites. For each,
say what it is and how a business usually gets listed.
```

## 7.1 Weekly profile update

```text
Write a Google Business Profile update (under 1,000 characters) for a
[TYPE OF BUSINESS] in [CITY]. This week's topic: [SERVICE / TIP / AREA].
Plain, friendly language, one clear call to action (call or book).
Use only these facts: [FACTS]. No prices, offers or claims I didn't give you.
```

## 7.3 Review request and reply templates

Template 1 · after the job (text message)

```text
Hi [first name], thanks for choosing [Business name] today. If you have a minute, a Google review helps other people nearby find us: [review link]
Thank you, [Your name]
```

Template 2 · by email (subject: How did we do?)

```text
Hi [first name],
Thanks again for [the job / your visit] on [day]. We're a small local business, and reviews are how people nearby decide whether to call us.
If you have two minutes, would you leave an honest review? [review link]
Thank you, [Your name], [Business name]
```

Template 3 · reply to a review

```text
Good review: Thanks, [first name]. Glad the [service] went well, and thank you for taking the time to write this. [Your name]
Critical review: Sorry this wasn't right, [first name]. I'd like to sort it out. Please call me on [phone] so we can talk it through. [Your name]
```

Keep replies short and calm, and never share a customer's details in public. Never offer anything in exchange for a review.
