## 5.3 Topical authority: services page

```text
You are an experienced local SEO copywriter who specialises in
[TYPE OF BUSINESS] businesses. You write engaging, trustworthy content
that ranks well and actually gets read by real people.

Task: Create a high-value services page (1,500–2,500 words) to build
topical authority around the core topic: " [TOPIC, e.g. SERVICES THAT OUR
SKINCARE CLINIC IN LOCATION OFFERS] ".

Target audience: [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE].

Search intent: Primarily informational with some commercial. People
want to discover the best options and the different services, find top
local recommendations, and get practical tips.

Primary keyword: " [PRIMARY KEYWORD] "

Secondary / related keywords to include naturally: [SECONDARY
KEYWORDS, if you have them]

Instructions:
1. Start with a short summary of the search intent and why this page
will satisfy it better than competitors.
2. Build a clear, scannable outline (H1–H3) that covers the topic
logically: intro -> key concepts -> local scene -> recommendations ->
tips -> FAQs.
3. Show expertise (E-E-A-T): include practical insights, mention real
local places and entities, and add unique tips or a simple checklist.
4. Structure the article:
- Hook + promise in the intro
- Main sections with short paragraphs, bullets, numbered lists and
1–2 small tables
- Internal linking: suggest 4–7 natural places to link to supporting
pages, with example anchor text
- FAQ section (6–10 common questions)
- Friendly conclusion + call to action (visit us, book, etc.)
5. Tone: warm, expert but approachable, conversational, like chatting
with a knowledgeable expert. Human-sounding, no fluff or repetition.
6. At the end, add:
- Suggested SEO title (60–120 characters)
- Meta description (150–160 characters)
- H1 suggestion
- 3–4 image alt text ideas (local-flavoured)
- Brief LocalBusiness or FAQ schema outline

Output format:
## Quick Strategy Summary
## Outline
## Full Article
## SEO Elements
Read the output before publishing: check every fact, price and claim, and remove anything that isn't true for your business.
```

## 5.4 Local authority: area page

```text
You are a senior local SEO specialist who ranks single-location
businesses in competitive cities by creating hyperlocal landing pages
for neighbourhood-specific searches.

Task: Write a conversion-focused, SEO-optimised neighbourhood landing page
(1,200–2,200 words) for our [TYPE OF BUSINESS] " [BUSINESS NAME] ",
located in [LOCATION] and serving [AREA 1].

Page goal: rank for queries like "[service] [neighbourhood]",
"best [service] in [neighbourhood]", "[service] near [landmark]".

Primary keyword: " [PRIMARY KEYWORD] "

Key business facts to weave in naturally:
- Physical address: [FULL NAP]
- Distance / proximity: [e.g. 10 minutes by car from the main square]
- Unique selling points: [LIST YOUR REAL SELLING POINTS]
- Local tie-ins: mention neighbourhood-specific landmarks, events and
pain points (e.g. busy market mornings, limited parking).

Instructions:
1. First, analyse hyperlocal intent: what do searchers in this
neighbourhood really want? How can we prove relevance without
claiming a fake address?
2. Create a scannable outline (H1–H3) that feels local-first:
neighbourhood intro -> why we're right for locals here ->
services tailored to the area -> directions / proof -> FAQs -> CTA.
3. Build local authority and E-E-A-T:
- Use real neighbourhood references (streets, markets, events,
history)
- Include proximity proof + a Google Maps embed suggestion
- Mention local customer types and needs
- Use recent local facts or trends only if you can state the
source; otherwise leave them out
4. Structure:
- Compelling H1 + introduction with a local hook
- Sections on services tailored to this area
- "Why locals choose us" as bullets. Use real customer quotes only
if I provide them below; never invent reviews or testimonials.
- Directions + transport tips from key local spots
- Embedded map + "Get directions" CTA suggestion
- 8–10 local-flavoured FAQs (e.g. "Is there parking near [street]?")
- Strong CTA: call, book, visit
5. On-page SEO at the end:
- SEO title and meta description (hyperlocal + compelling)
- Suggested schema: LocalBusiness + areaServed including the
neighbourhood
- 4–6 image alt texts with local keywords
- Internal linking: 5–7 suggestions to services, other
neighbourhoods and key pages
6. Tone: warm, community-oriented, expert but neighbourly. Human,
trustworthy voice, no generic corporate speak.

Customer quotes I can use (optional): [PASTE REAL QUOTES OR "NONE"]

Output format:
## Hyperlocal Intent Analysis
## Page Outline
## Full Page Content
## On-Page SEO & Schema Recommendations
```
